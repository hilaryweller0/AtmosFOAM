I created field ghGradh to simplify your code.

You calculate phi at the very start and end of the time step. You don't need both. I removed the one at the start.

I reduced the number of non-linear iterations. They weren't all needed.

I removed phi_a and U_a for the second implicit non-linear advection and did the calculation more efficiently. 

I calculate hf before hEqn rather than after to make sure that the same hf is used in ghGradh (for consistency).
